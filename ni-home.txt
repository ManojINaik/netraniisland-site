How much does it cost to go to Netrani Island?
Netrani Island price depends on whether you book snorkeling, scuba diving, a private boat, or a custom group trip from Murudeshwar. Shared snorkeling starts from the published per-person rate, while scuba and private boats are quoted by plan.
How do I go to Netrani Island from Murudeshwar?
Reach Murudeshwar first, report near the beach, and board the morning boat. The crossing usually takes about 45 minutes to 1 hour each way.
Is it safe for first-time visitors?
Yes, many first-time visitors join with instructor guidance, but weather, health condition, and crew instructions still matter for final approval.
How early should I book?
Book early for weekends, holidays, and private boats. If your date is fixed, message the team in advance to check seat or boat availability.
