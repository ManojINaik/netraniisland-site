Frequently Asked Questions
How many days are sufficient for Murudeshwar trip?
A 2-day trip is ideal for Murudeshwar. Day 1: Scuba diving at Netrani Island (takes full day). Day 2: Temple darshan, Shiva statue visit, and beach activities. You can add one more day for nearby Gokarna.
Do you offer packages including Gokarna and Honnavar?
Yes, we offer combo packages covering Murudeshwar, Eco Beach Honnavar (Mangrove boardwalk), and Gokarna beaches. Custom vehicle rental is included.
