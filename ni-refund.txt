Refund and Rescheduling Policy
Last updated: March 15, 2026. Trip changes depend on sea state, boat allocation, and the amount of notice given before departure.
Weather and sea-condition changes
If sea conditions make a trip unsafe, we prioritise rescheduling to the next workable departure. Weather-based changes are handled without penalty to the guest for the affected booking window.
Guest reschedules
If your date changes, contact the booking desk as early as possible. Approval depends on notice, boat allocation, season demand, and whether guides or private boats were reserved specifically for your group.
No-shows and late arrivals
Trips operate on morning reporting windows. If a guest misses the reporting time or fails to arrive after confirmation, refunds are not guaranteed because the seat, boat, and crew capacity were already held.
Private boats and large groups
Private-charter and large-group bookings require tighter scheduling and usually involve higher coordination cost. Those plans may have stricter refund or transfer rules than shared individual bookings.
