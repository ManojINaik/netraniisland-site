Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about your trip to Murudeshwar.
What types of accommodations are available in Murudeshwar?
Book Murudeshwar helps you request sea view rooms, temple view rooms, AC or non-AC rooms, budget lodges, resorts and family homestays. Share your dates and requirements so our team can check availability, facilities and the full price before you book.
How do I book scuba diving packages in Murudeshwar?
You can book scuba diving packages through our website by contacting us via WhatsApp at +91 7829008264 or through our contact form. We offer both beginner and advanced scuba diving packages at Netrani Island with PADI certified instructors.
What is included in the Family Saver Bundle package?
The Family Saver Bundle includes Sea/Temple View Rooms (AC/Non-AC options), vehicle rental services, and an optional beginner-friendly scuba diving add-on. This package is designed to provide the best value for families at local rates.
Are vehicle rentals available in Murudeshwar?
Yes, we provide vehicle rental services including two-wheelers, cars, and tempo travellers at the best local rates. Vehicles can be delivered to your accommodation with quick paperwork processing.
What is the best time to visit Murudeshwar for scuba diving?
The best time for scuba diving at Netrani Island is from October to May when water conditions are optimal with visibility ranging from 15-20 meters. During this period, the sea is calmer and marine life is most active.
Are rooms available with shared kitchen facilities?
Yes, select accommodations offer shared guest kitchen facilities, which are perfect for families with specific dietary needs or those who prefer to prepare their own meals. We also provide access to trusted Pure Veg and Non-Veg restaurants nearby.
How far is the accommodation from Murudeshwar Temple?
Our accommodations are strategically located near both the beach and Murudeshwar Temple. Most properties are within walking distance or a short drive from the temple, ensuring convenient access to this iconic spiritual landmark.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept multiple payment methods including Cash, Credit Cards, UPI, and Bank Transfers for your convenience. All transactions are secure and processed professionally.
How to reach Murudeshwar from Bangalore or Mumbai?
From Bangalore (480 km): Take NH48 via Tumkur-Chitradurga-Haveri-Honnavar to Murudeshwar (8-9 hours by car). Direct buses run from Majestic Bus Stand. From Mumbai (500 km): Take NH66 coastal highway via Goa-Gokarna to Murudeshwar (10-11 hours). The nearest railway station is Murudeshwar on Konkan Railway with direct trains from Mumbai, Mangalore, and Goa.
What are Murudeshwar Temple timings?
Murudeshwar Temple is open from 5:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily. The best times to visit are early morning (5-7 AM) for peaceful darshan and sunset time (5-7 PM) for beautiful sea views. The Shiva statue and gopuram elevator operate from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM with a small entry fee.
Is Murudeshwar safe for families and solo travelers?
Yes, Murudeshwar is very safe for families and solo travelers. It's a peaceful temple town with friendly locals. The beach area is well-maintained with lifeguards on duty. We recommend booking accommodations near the temple area for added security and convenience.
What is the best season to visit Murudeshwar?
The best time to visit Murudeshwar is October to March when weather is pleasant (23-32°C) and ideal for beach activities and scuba diving. Monsoon season (June-September) sees heavy rainfall but offers lush green scenery. Summer (April-May) is hot but less crowded with good hotel deals.
Are there good vegetarian restaurants in Murudeshwar?
Yes, Murudeshwar has many excellent vegetarian restaurants serving South Indian, North Indian, and local Malnad cuisine. Popular options include hotels near the temple serving thali meals and dosa varieties. We can recommend trusted Pure Veg restaurants based on your preferences.
Where can I find rooms in Murudeshwar?

What are the best lodges in Murudeshwar?
The right lodge depends on your budget, arrival time and temple travel plans. Ask our local team to check the room category, bathroom, hot water, parking, walking route and total price for your dates. Confirm these details before paying.
How to book a room in Murudeshwar online?
Choose a stay category and complete the room enquiry form, or WhatsApp Book Murudeshwar on +91 7829008264. Share check-in, check-out, guests, rooms and budget. Our team helps check options; confirm the room, full price and booking terms before making a payment.

You can request budget hotels, lodges, AC rooms, beach or sea view stays and family homestays through Book Murudeshwar. Rates depend on dates, occupancy, location and facilities.
