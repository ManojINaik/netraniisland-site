Netrani Island Travel Guide: Distance, Timings, Season, and Ground Rules

Netrani Island, also called Pigeon Island, is a small, uninhabited coral island in the Arabian Sea, about 19 km (10 nautical miles) off Murudeshwar in Bhatkal taluk, Uttara Kannada, Karnataka. From the air it looks heart-shaped. It is known for scuba diving and snorkelling over its reefs, and boats from Murudeshwar reach it in about 45–90 minutes. Public landing on Netrani Island is prohibited because it is a restricted naval area. Karnataka Tourism’s Netrani Island guide

The season runs roughly from October to May, and November to February is the most popular time to go. Boats usually stop during the monsoon, from June to September.

What happens on a Netrani boat trip?

  1. Check in at the operator’s meeting point in Murudeshwar. Ask for the map pin and reporting time when you book. Confirm both and check that your boat is expected to sail the evening before.
  2. Cross to the reefs after check-in. We use 45–60 minutes each way as a rough estimate for some trips. Karnataka Tourism gives about 90 minutes for the boat ride. Ask your operator how long to allow for your crossing. Karnataka Tourism
  3. Leave time for the afternoon return. The boat returns after the group finishes at the reef. Check the expected return with your operator and leave a buffer before any onward train or bus.

Why Visit Netrani Island?

Netrani Island, also spelled Nethrani or Nethravathi, is one of Karnataka's best-known offshore diving locations. What you see depends on the date, sea state and dive site; the following are possibilities reported by dive content, not promises:

  • Visibility: Can be clear in suitable conditions, but no visibility number is guaranteed
  • Marine Life: Reef fish and other marine life may be seen; no species or whale-shark sighting is guaranteed
  • Corals: Vibrant ecosystem of soft and hard corals
  • Dolphins: Sometimes reported during boat journeys, never guaranteed
  • Adventure: The 1-hour boat ride through open sea is thrilling itself

Marine Life at Netrani Island

Reef fish and coral are part of Netrani's appeal. The animals below appear in dive and travel reports, but encounters depend on the site and conditions. Ask your guide what has been seen recently and enjoy the dive without a fixed sighting checklist.

Reef Fish and Other Marine Life

  • Butterfly Fish — colorful reef dwellers
  • Trigger Fish — distinctive reef fish to observe from a respectful distance
  • Parrot Fish — large, brightly colored, visible near coral structures
  • Moray Eels — look for them around rock crevices with your guide
  • Nudibranchs — tiny colorful sea slugs, a favorite for macro photography
  • Lion Fish — distinctive fan-like fins, usually near ledges
  • Sea Cucumbers — on the sandy bottom between coral patches

Seasonal Highlights (November - February)

These are reported possibilities rather than a forecast for November-February or any other date.

  • Whale sharks — occasionally mentioned in older dive/travel content; no current source lets this page predict a sighting or reliable month.
  • Manta rays — possible in regional dive reports, but not guaranteed at any depth or date.
  • Dolphins — sometimes reported during the boat ride; never plan the trip around a promised sighting.
  • Large Schools of Trevally — hunting formations near the reef wall

Coral System

Netrani dive reports describe both hard and soft coral formations. Ask your instructor about the chosen site's depth, current and suitability for your experience; one side of the island should not be assumed calmer on every trip. Keep clear of coral and follow the briefing on buoyancy and where to place your hands and fins.

What to Carry?

  1. Sunscreen & Hat: The sun is harsh in open sea.
  2. Seasickness Planning: If you are prone to seasickness, seek medical advice before travelling and tell the operator about any medicine you use. DAN explains that some medicines can cause drowsiness that affects diving.
  3. Towel & Change of Clothes: You will get wet!
  4. Water & Snacks: Light snacks are usually provided, but carry water.

Is scuba diving at Netrani Island safe?

Safety depends on the named operator, instructor/dive master, equipment, participant screening, training and sea conditions. A PADI directory verifies at least one local PADI Dive Center, but this page cannot claim every operator or every piece of equipment meets the same standard. Verify before booking.

Can children go to Netrani Island?

PADI Discover Scuba guidance sets a minimum age of 10, but age alone does not establish eligibility. Confirm the programme, medical screening, water comfort and supervision for your child. Passenger and snorkeling places also depend on the operator's age, boarding and activity requirements.

Is pre-booking required for a Netrani Island trip?

Confirm your place before travelling, especially when your dates are fixed. Ask how many people your group can book together, the payment deadline and what happens if sea conditions cancel the trip.

Can we land on Netrani Island?

No. The island is a restricted naval area. Divers and snorkellers enter the water from the boat near the reef, and sightseeing passengers stay on board.

Which month is best to visit Netrani Island?

November to February is the most popular time. The season runs roughly from October to May, and Karnataka Tourism recommends November to May. Boats usually stop during the monsoon, from June to September. Sea conditions change daily, so confirm your date with the operator.

How much does it cost to visit Netrani Island?

With Book Murudeshwar, a boat-only seat to Netrani Island costs ₹1,999 per person and beginner scuba diving costs ₹3,499 per person, including the return boat, scuba gear, an instructor-guided dive and underwater photos. Snorkelling is usually ₹1,500–2,000 per person. Meals and hotel pickup are extra.

What can you do at Netrani Island?

You can scuba dive, snorkel or ride along on the boat as a sightseeing passenger. All three happen from the boat beside the reef, because nobody is allowed to land on the island. Most trips leave Murudeshwar in the morning and return by afternoon.

Why is Netrani Island called Pigeon Island?

The name comes from the pigeons that live on the island. It is also known for its heart shape when seen from the air, and the name is sometimes spelled Nethrani.
