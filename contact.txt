Use WhatsApp when you already know the basics
It is the easiest way to send your travel date, guest count, and preferred activity in one place, especially for families, private boats, and group bookings.

## What Happens After You Contact Us
1. We understand your trip plan
We look at your date, group size, and whether you want scuba diving, snorkeling, a private boat, or a larger group arrangement.
2. We guide you to the right option
If you are not sure what fits, we can point you to the right package, explain shared versus private boat options, and tell you what information we still need.
3. You confirm and prepare for departure
Once your plan is clear, we share the next booking steps and remind you that all trips depart from Murudeshwar for same-day return.
## Best time to message before your date fills
Shared snorkeling or scuba
A few days ahead is usually workable on quieter weekdays, but weekend slots fill faster once sea conditions look good.
Private boat plans
Private departures are easier to shape when you contact us 7 to 10 days ahead with the group size and preferred timing window.
Large groups or resort guests
The earlier the better. Big groups usually need extra boat matching, beginner briefing support, and payment coordination before the date is secure.
## Service Area
All trips depart from Murudeshwar Beach, Karnataka and go to Netrani Island (also called Pigeon Island). We serve visitors from across Karnataka and India. Same-day departure and return — no overnight stay needed.
Daily booking support available via phone and WhatsApp.
