Distance and time
Hubli Airport to Murudeshwar distance: about 205 km, not 160
Some older pages list Hubli as 155 to 160 km from Murudeshwar. By road it is about 205 km from Hubballi Airport (HBX), and Hubballi city is about the same. Plan for 4 to 4.5 hours with one break. The drive is shorter on the map than it feels, because a good part of it is through the forested Western Ghats.
Coming from elsewhere in North Karnataka? By road, Haveri to Murudeshwar is about 180 km (3.5 to 4 hours, usually via Sirsi and Kumta), Belagavi is about 285 km and Davanagere about 270 km. We arrange pickups from these towns too: send your pickup point and date on WhatsApp for a quote.
Route options
Via Yellapur, via Sirsi, or round by Karwar
There are three practical ways down to the coast, and the driver picks one based on the season, road works and your stops.
Via Kalghatgi and Yellapur, then down the ghat towards Kumta and south on NH66. The usual and shortest choice, about 205 km.
Via Sirsi and the Devimane ghat to Kumta. About 215 km. Useful if you want to visit Sirsi Marikamba Temple.
Via Yellapur and Ankola, joining NH66 near Karwar and driving south past Gokarna and Kumta. About 225 km, with fewer tight hairpins. Sometimes preferred in heavy monsoon.
Road conditions
Ghat roads and monsoon caution
The ghat sections have sharp bends, slow trucks and, from June to September, heavy rain, fog and the occasional landslide or tree fall that closes a road for a few hours. The drivers we work with know these roads and switch routes when one is blocked, but it is wise to keep the plan relaxed in the monsoon rather than chasing a tight arrival time.
If you land in the evening, the ghat portion will be in the dark. Some travellers prefer to stay a night in Hubballi and leave in the morning, especially in the rainy season or with young children. If anyone gets motion sickness, sit in front and eat lightly before the ghat.
After you land
How the Hubli airport pickup works
Share your flight number, landing time, passengers, bags and the drop point in Murudeshwar. We follow the flight, so a delay does not leave you stranded. The driver's name and phone number are shared before the day. Call them once you have your bags. Ola and Uber do not operate in Murudeshwar, so the onward local travel after you arrive also needs a local taxi, and we can arrange that too.
Going back
Murudeshwar to Hubli Airport: when to leave
For a flight from Hubballi, allow 4 to 4.5 hours of driving, an extra hour of buffer (more in the monsoon) and about 2 hours at the airport. In practice, leave Murudeshwar 7 to 7.5 hours before departure. Avoid planning the ghat section late at night if you can.
Compare airports
Hubli vs Mangaluru vs Goa for Murudeshwar
Mangaluru (IXE, about 156 km on the flat coastal highway) is usually the easiest airport for Murudeshwar. Hubballi makes sense when flights from your city connect better there, or when you are combining the trip with Sirsi, Yellapur or north Karnataka. Goa Dabolim is a similar distance (about 206 km) but takes longer along the coast.
Plan the rest
Book the room and scuba along with the transfer
After a long ghat drive, a ready room helps. Netrani boats run in the morning and depend on sea conditions, so most Hubballi arrivals dive the day after they land. Our team is based in Murudeshwar and can arrange the airport taxi, a room and the dive slot in one WhatsApp chat. Send your dates and we suggest a plan, with no payment needed to ask.

What is the Hubli Airport to Murudeshwar taxi fare?
A sedan starts at ₹4,000 one-way. SUV and Tempo Traveller fares are quoted on WhatsApp. Tolls, parking, night charges and waiting time are confirmed in your quote.
How far is Murudeshwar from Hubli?
About 205 km by road from Hubballi Airport or city, usually 4 to 4.5 hours. Older figures of 155 to 160 km are not correct by road.
Which route does the taxi take from Hubli to Murudeshwar?
Usually via Kalghatgi and Yellapur, down the ghat towards Kumta and then NH66. Alternatives are via Sirsi (about 215 km) or round by Ankola and Karwar (about 225 km).
Is the Hubli to Murudeshwar road safe in the monsoon?
It is driveable, but the ghats get heavy rain, fog and occasional closures from June to September. Travel in daylight where possible and keep buffer time.
What if my flight into Hubballi is delayed?
Share your flight number and we follow the status and adjust the pickup. Long waits are discussed and confirmed in the quote.
When should I leave Murudeshwar for a Hubli flight?
About 7 to 7.5 hours before departure, which covers the drive, an hour of buffer and time at the airport. Allow more in the monsoon.
Can we stop at Sirsi Marikamba Temple on the way?
Yes, by taking the Sirsi route. Add it when you book so the waiting time and the extra distance are in the quote.
Is Hubli or Mangaluru the better airport for Murudeshwar?
Mangaluru, at about 156 km on the flat coastal road, is usually easier. Hubballi suits you when flights connect better or you are visiting north Karnataka too.
