Goa to Murudeshwar and Murudeshwar to Goa: Distance, Train, Bus and Cab
Planning a trip from 
Goa to Murudeshwar
? The road distance is about 
142-238 km
, depending on where in Goa you start: about 
177 km from Madgaon
, 
206 km from Dabolim Airport
, 
210 km from Panaji
, 
220 km from Calangute/Baga
 and 
238 km from Mopa Airport
. Palolem and Canacona in South Goa are closest, at about 142 km. By road, most travelers take the NH66 coastal route and reach Murudeshwar in about 
3-5.5 hours of driving
, plus breaks.
Murudeshwar is not in Goa. It is a temple town in Bhatkal taluk, Uttara Kannada district, 
Karnataka
, south of Goa on the same coastal highway and railway line.
Route check (19 October 2026):
 We measured each Goa starting point by road routing to Murudeshwar Temple and cross-checked the results against published route sources. Your exact hotel or terminal pin can add a few kilometres. Confirm trains on 
IRCTC
 or 
NTES
 because running days and monsoon schedules can change.
The easiest route is 
Goa -> Canacona -> Karwar -> Ankola -> Kumta -> Honnavar -> Murudeshwar
. For the reverse journey, follow 
Murudeshwar -> Honnavar -> Kumta -> Ankola -> Karwar -> Canacona -> Goa
. If you prefer the train, use 
Madgaon Junction (MAO) and Murdeshwar/Murudeshwar Railway Station (MRDW)
. If you want a door-to-door trip with rooms, resorts, scuba diving, and local sightseeing included, a pre-planned taxi package is usually the smoothest option.
This guide is for travelers 
coming from Goa to Murudeshwar
. Heading back the other way? The 
Murudeshwar to Goa distance
 page covers departure trains, Goa drop points and airport drops from Murudeshwar.

Goa to Murudeshwar and Murudeshwar to Goa Distance at a Glance
The distance changes because Goa has multiple tourist zones and two major airports. South Goa and Dabolim are closer to Murudeshwar than North Goa and Mopa. The reverse-route distance is the same corridor, but your final drop in Goa decides the actual travel time.

Nearest Railway Station to Murudeshwar Temple
The nearest railway station to Murudeshwar Temple is 
Murudeshwar Railway Station (MRDW)
. It is the right station for the temple, beach, Shiva statue, hotels near the temple, sea-view rooms, and most local transfers.
Do not confuse this with Bhatkal or Mangalore if your main plan is Murudeshwar Temple. Bhatkal can work for some routes, but MRDW is the most convenient arrival point when trains are available.

Best Travel Tips from Locals
Start early from Goa if you are driving or taking a cab.
Keep your pickup point specific: North Goa, Panjim, Dabolim, Mopa, Madgaon, or South Goa.
Check train timings live before booking because schedules and operating days can change.
Confirm your room before arrival during weekends, holidays, and December-January peak season.
If scuba is part of your plan, reach Murudeshwar one day before the dive.
Add Gokarna only if you have enough time; otherwise, the direct NH66 route is simpler.
Carry cash for autos, snacks, parking, and small local expenses.
Keep temple clothing simple and comfortable, especially if you are arriving directly from Goa.

Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
Quick answers for this guide, based on the most common traveler queries.
What is the distance from Goa to Murudeshwar?
The Goa to Murudeshwar distance is about 177-238 km by road from Goa's main towns and airports: Madgaon about 177 km, Dabolim about 206 km, Panaji about 210 km, Calangute/Baga about 220 km and Mopa about 238 km. Palolem and Canacona in South Goa are closest, at about 142 km.
Is Murudeshwar in Goa?
No. Murudeshwar is in Karnataka, in Bhatkal taluk of Uttara Kannada district. It lies south of Goa on the NH66 coast, about 177-238 km from Goa's main towns and airports and about 142 km from Palolem at Goa's southern edge.
What is the Murudeshwar to Goa distance?
The Murudeshwar to Goa distance is the same road in reverse: about 177 km to Madgaon, 206 km to Dabolim, 210 km to Panaji and 238 km to Mopa Airport. See the Murudeshwar to Goa distance page for departure trains and drop planning.
How long does it take from Goa to Murudeshwar by cab?
Most Goa to Murudeshwar cab trips take about 3-5.5 hours of driving: about 3.5-4 hours from Madgaon, 4.5-5 hours from Dabolim or Panaji and 5-5.5 hours from Mopa, plus breaks. Travel time increases if you add Gokarna or travel in heavy traffic.
Is there a train from Goa to Murudeshwar?
Yes. The practical train route is Madgaon Junction (MAO) to Murudeshwar/Murdeshwar Railway Station (MRDW), about 2.5-3 hours. The daily 12619 Matsyagandha Express arrives at Murdeshwar at 05:34 and the daily 16345 Netravati Express at 02:20. Always check live timings on IRCTC or NTES before booking.
What time does the Goa to Murudeshwar train arrive?
The two regular daily trains arrive before sunrise: Netravati Express at 02:20 and Matsyagandha Express at 05:34. Pre-book a station pickup and ask for early check-in or a room from the previous night, so you are not waiting at the station.
Is bus better or train better from Goa to Murudeshwar?
Train is usually faster if you are near Madgaon and the timing works. Bus can be cheaper or more flexible for some pickup points, but it may involve transfers or longer travel time.
Which is better: Goa to Murudeshwar taxi or train?
Choose train for budget travel. Choose taxi if you want door-to-door pickup, airport transfer, luggage comfort, resort drop, Gokarna stop, or scuba timing support.
Is there a Murudeshwar to Goa train?
Yes. The 12620 Matsyagandha Express leaves Murdeshwar (MRDW) daily at 15:48 for Madgaon, and the 16346 Netravati Express leaves daily at 01:00 for Madgaon, Karmali and Thivim. Check live train timings before planning your return, especially if your final Goa drop is Panjim, North Goa, Dabolim Airport, or Mopa Airport.
Can I do Goa to Murudeshwar as a day trip?
It is possible but tiring. A day trip works best by private cab with an early start. For temple, beach, rooms, resort stay, or scuba diving, a 1-night or 2-night plan is better.
Can I add scuba diving to a Goa to Murudeshwar trip?
Yes. Add scuba only if you can reach Murudeshwar in time for rest and early reporting. A scuba + stay combo is easier than trying to arrive and dive on the same morning.
Where should I stay in Murudeshwar after coming from Goa?
Choose temple-area rooms for darshan, sea-view rooms for views, resorts for comfort, homestays for family-style stays, and scuba-friendly stays if Netrani Island is your main activity.
