Scuba Diving in Murudeshwar: Price, Inclusions and Booking
Scuba diving in Murudeshwar happens at Netrani Island, about 19 km offshore by boat. With
Scuba Murudeshwar
, the
Netrani Island scuba diving
package costs 
₹3,500 per person
 and includes the boat ride, scuba gear, a briefing with shallow-water practice, an instructor-guided dive, and underwater photos and videos. Boats leave between 8:00 AM and 9:00 AM in the October to May season, and the usual starting age is 10. To book, WhatsApp or call Scuba Murudeshwar on

When is the best time to visit for scuba diving in Murudeshwar?
The 
best time for scuba diving in Murudeshwar
 is from 
October to May
. During this 
Murudeshwar scuba diving season
, water visibility at Netrani Island is clearer in season (15-30 metres). Note: Diving is closed during the monsoon (June to September).

Is there an age limit for scuba diving in India?
Yes, the 
scuba diving age limit
 is 10 years and above for most courses. However, for the beginner scuba dive at Netrani, anyone above 10 years who is medically fit can participate. There is no strict 
scuba diving maximum age
 as long as you are healthy.

Can non-swimmers do scuba diving in Murudeshwar?
Non-swimmers may be able to try scuba diving in Murudeshwar
 with the
 instructor-guided beginner dive
, after medical and water-comfort checks. You must be comfortable in the water and able to maintain yourself at the surface. The dive team makes the final suitability decision.

Boats for 
Netrani Island scuba diving
 depart daily between 
8:00 AM and 9:00 AM
 from Murudeshwar beach. The trip typically lasts until 3:00 PM. We recommend 
booking

How do I reach Murudeshwar from Bangalore or Mangalore?
Most guests travel overnight by bus or train to reach Murudeshwar early morning for the boat. Self-drive routes via NH 66 are also common. If you're planning a weekend trip, an overnight stay makes the dive day stress-free.

Is it "Murudeshwar" or "Murdeshwar" scuba diving?
Both spellings refer to the same place! The correct spelling is 
Murudeshwar
 (ಮುರುಡೇಶ್ವರ), but it is commonly written as 
Murdeshwar
, Murdeshvar, or Murudeshwara. No matter how you spell it, the 
scuba diving experience at Netrani Island
 remains the same — Netrani Island scuba diving for ₹3,500 per person.
