After you land
How the airport pickup works after your flight lands
Mangaluru International Airport (IXE) is at Bajpe, north-east of Mangaluru city. Send us your flight number, landing time, number of passengers and bags, and your drop point in Murudeshwar. We share the driver's name and phone number before the day of travel.
We check your flight status, so a delayed landing does not leave you without a car. Once you collect your bags, call or WhatsApp the driver and walk out to the arrivals pickup area. If you are carrying dive gear, a pram or a lot of luggage, tell us in advance so we send a vehicle with enough boot space.
Share the flight number, not only the time. Delays are easier to follow.
Tell us about elders, children or extra bags so the vehicle fits.
Give the exact drop: hotel name, temple side, beach side or railway station.
The drive
Mangalore Airport to Murudeshwar route: 156 km via Udupi, Kundapura and Byndoor
From the airport the car joins NH66 near Mulki and stays on the coastal highway all the way north: Udupi, Brahmavar, Kundapura, Maravanthe, Byndoor, Bhatkal and then Murudeshwar. The road distance is about 156 km (Mangaluru city is about 159 km) and a realistic drive time is 3 to 3.5 hours with one short break.
Traffic is usually slowest through Udupi and Kundapura in the late afternoon. In the monsoon, rain and road works can add time, so we plan arrivals with a little buffer instead of promising a tight time.
Not flying in?
Mangalore to Murudeshwar taxi fare from the city and railway station
Mangaluru city is about 159 km from Murudeshwar Temple, a 3 to 3.5 hour drive on the same NH66 coastal highway. We pick up from hotels in the city and from Mangaluru Central and Mangaluru Junction railway stations as well as the airport.
The published sedan fare of ₹4,500 is for the airport pickup. A pickup from the city or a railway station is quoted on WhatsApp once you share the exact point, the time and the number of passengers. Tolls, parking, night charges and waiting time are confirmed in your quote.

By rail
Trains to Murdeshwar from Mangaluru
The daytime option is the 16585 Express: Mangaluru Central 08:40, Udupi 10:42 and Murdeshwar 13:50 (confirm on NTES). It reaches Murdeshwar (MRDW) in the early afternoon, which leaves time to check in, see the temple in the evening and dive at Netrani the next morning.
The coastal Madgaon-Mangaluru passenger and MEMU trains, the Matsyagandha Express and the Netravati Express also stop at Murdeshwar. Their timings vary, so check NTES for the day you travel. Murdeshwar station is 1.5-2 km from the temple and an auto costs ₹50-80.
By bus
KSRTC and private buses along NH66
KSRTC and private buses between Mangaluru and Bhatkal, Murudeshwar and towns further north are frequent on NH66. Timings and fares change, so check KSRTC or redBus for timings before you travel. Some buses drop on the highway rather than in town, so ask the conductor for the Murudeshwar stop and keep a short auto ride in mind.
Optional stops
Stops worth adding: Udupi Krishna Temple and Maravanthe beach
If you land in the morning, the transfer can double as a short sightseeing drive. Add the stops when you ask for the quote so the waiting time is clear upfront.
Udupi Sri Krishna Temple, a short detour from the highway in Udupi city, plus a meal at one of Udupi's well-known vegetarian restaurants.
Malpe beach, about 6 km west of Udupi, if you have a couple of extra hours.
Maravanthe, where NH66 runs between the sea and the Sowparnika river. A quick photo stop between Kundapura and Byndoor.
Late and early flights
Late-night arrivals and early-morning departures
Many flights into Mangaluru land in the evening or late at night. Public buses towards Bhatkal and Murudeshwar thin out at night, and Ola and Uber do not operate in Murudeshwar, so a pre-booked taxi avoids being stuck at the airport. Night charges, if any, are written into your quote before you confirm. You never hear about them for the first time from the driver.
Arriving after midnight? Tell us your hotel name too. We can let the hotel know you are arriving late, or suggest a stay that allows late check-in.
Going back
Murudeshwar to Mangalore Airport: when to leave for your flight
For the return, work backwards from your departure time. Allow 3 to 3.5 hours for the drive, about an hour of buffer for traffic, rain or a slow stretch near Udupi, and around 2 hours at the airport for check-in and security. In practice that means leaving Murudeshwar at least 5.5 to 6 hours before your flight's departure time.
If your flight is early in the morning, it is often easier to start the night before and rest near the airport, or to leave Murudeshwar in the small hours with the night charge confirmed in advance. Tell us the flight time and we suggest a sensible pickup time.
Compare airports
Nearest airports to Murudeshwar compared
Mangaluru is usually the most convenient airport for Murudeshwar. It is the shortest drive and the whole route is on one highway. Hubballi is a similar distance to Goa Dabolim but crosses a ghat section. Goa works well if you are already holidaying there or flights from your city are better.
Mangaluru (IXE): about 156 km, 3-3.5 hours, sedan from ₹4,500.
Hubballi (HBX): about 205 km, 4-4.5 hours via a ghat road, sedan from ₹4,000.
Goa Dabolim (GOI): about 206 km, 4.5-5 hours along the coast, sedan from ₹5,500.
Goa Mopa (GOX): about 238 km, 5-5.5 hours, quoted on WhatsApp.
Plan the rest
Land, check in, and dive the next morning
A common plan is to land at Mangaluru, reach Murudeshwar by evening, and dive at Netrani the next morning. Netrani boats run in the morning and depend on sea conditions, so booking the dive slot together with the airport taxi and a room near the reporting point keeps the first day simple. Send your dates on WhatsApp and we suggest a plan, with no payment needed to ask.
