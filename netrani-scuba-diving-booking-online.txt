# Netrani Scuba Diving Booking Online
Online booking works best after you understand the main scuba trip and pricing options. Use this support guide to make your WhatsApp message clear, avoid back-and-forth delays, and confirm the details that matter before payment.
## What to send in your first message
→ Preferred date (and a backup date if possible)
→ Total guest count and first-time diver count
→ Activity preference (scuba, snorkeling, or mixed)
→ Whether you need shared seats or private boat options
## The booking flow in plain steps
Step 1
Share your date and guest count
Start with your preferred date, number of people, and whether your group includes first-time divers.
Step 2
Choose the right trip format
The team helps you decide between shared seats, private boat, or mixed activity plans based on your group style.
Step 3
Confirm slot and reporting details
Before payment, confirm reporting point, timing window, and what to carry so there are no surprises on trip day.
Step 4
Get final booking confirmation
Once details are locked, you receive booking confirmation and prep notes for the Murudeshwar departure.
## Why bookings fail and how to avoid it
Incomplete guest details
When age and experience details are skipped, quote and safety checks take longer than needed.
No timing flexibility
Strict single-slot planning leaves no room for sea-condition adjustments or operational windows.
Price-only decisions
The lowest number is not always the best trip fit. Read format and timing along with price.
