The drive
Murudeshwar to Gokarna route: 77 km on NH66 via Honnavar and Kumta
The road distance from Murudeshwar Temple to Gokarna is about 77 km and the drive usually takes 1.5 to 2 hours. Almost the whole way is on NH66, the coastal highway. You head north through Manki to Honnavar (about 26 km), cross the long Sharavathi river bridge, continue to Kumta (about 45 km from Murudeshwar), and then turn off the highway for the last stretch into Gokarna town.
The highway is a good two-lane-plus road for most of the way, but expect slow patches through Honnavar and Kumta town and at a few bridge works. In the monsoon (June to September) heavy rain can add 20 to 30 minutes, so drivers keep a steady pace instead of rushing.
Worth stopping
Stops on the way: Apsarakonda Falls, Kumta and Mirjan Fort
Because the drive is short, it is easy to add one or two stops without making the day tiring. Tell us which ones you want when you ask for the quote, so the waiting time is included and there is no discussion with the driver later.
Apsarakonda Falls and the beach beside it, a short detour just north of Honnavar, good for a 30 to 45 minute break.
Honnavar riverside on the Sharavathi for photos from the bridge approach.
Kumta town for breakfast, tea or a fuel stop roughly halfway.
Mirjan Fort, a laterite fort a few kilometres off NH66 between Kumta and the Gokarna turn-off.
Where to get dropped
Gokarna drop points: town, Kudle Beach, Om Beach or Gokarna Road station
Gokarna is spread out, so the exact drop point matters more than the town name. Tell us the name of your stay or the beach you want and we quote for that point.
Gokarna Road railway station is not in Gokarna town. It is roughly 10 km away on the highway side, so if you are catching a Konkan Railway train, mention the station and train time separately from any beach drop.
Gokarna town and Mahabaleshwar Temple: the car drops you near the temple streets, which are narrow and busy at festival times.
Kudle Beach: the car stops at the top of the cliff road and you walk down the steps with your bags.
Om Beach: there is road access and parking close to the beach. Half Moon and Paradise beaches are reached on foot or by boat from here, not by car.
Gokarna Road station: share the train number so the driver plans for platform timing, not beach timing.
One-way or return
One-way drop vs same-day return with waiting
Choose a one-way drop if you are moving your stay to Gokarna. The published ₹2,000 sedan and ₹2,800 SUV fares are for this. If you want to see Gokarna and sleep in Murudeshwar again, ask for a same-day return: the driver waits while you visit the temple and beaches and brings you back. The quote then covers the return kilometres and the hours of waiting, and you know the total before you confirm.
Travelling the other way, Gokarna to Murudeshwar, works the same. Send your Gokarna pickup point and time and we confirm the fare. Ola and Uber do not operate in Murudeshwar, so booking the return leg in advance is safer than hoping to find a cab on the day.
Timing tips
When to leave for Gokarna and when to come back
For a day trip, leaving Murudeshwar by 7:30 or 8 AM gets you to Gokarna before the midday heat, with time for darshan and two beaches. Start back by 4:30 or 5 PM so most of the drive is in daylight. If you want sunset at Om Beach or Kudle, plan a later return and tell us in advance, because night charges are confirmed in your quote.
If you are diving at Netrani, keep Gokarna for a different day. Boats run in the morning and depend on sea conditions, so a dive morning and a Gokarna day rarely fit together comfortably.
Netrani dive day
Gokarna to Murudeshwar transfer for Netrani scuba diving
Netrani dives leave from Murudeshwar in the morning, and Gokarna is about 77 km away, so a dive day needs an early transfer. The easiest plan is to take the taxi the afternoon or evening before and sleep in Murudeshwar, so you only walk to the reporting point. If you prefer to stay in Gokarna, send us your operator's reporting time and we set the pickup with 1.5 to 2 hours of driving plus a margin. Early pickups may carry night charges, which are confirmed in your quote.
Scuba diving starts at ₹3,499 per person, and a boat-only seat for a non-diving companion is ₹1,999. Our scuba diving near Gokarna guide, linked below, covers what the dive includes and how to plan it from Gokarna.
Plan the rest
Combine Gokarna with your Murudeshwar stay and scuba
Many travellers do Murudeshwar and Gokarna in one trip: arrive by train or flight, dive at Netrani on the first morning, then take a taxi to Gokarna or do it as a return day. Our team is based in Murudeshwar, so you can ask for the room, the dive slot and the Gokarna taxi in the same WhatsApp chat. Sea-view and temple-side rooms are limited and get busy on long weekends and from December to January, so it helps to share your dates early. Asking for a plan costs nothing.
Once you are in Murudeshwar, you can also rent a scooter or bike for the temple, beaches and nearby viewpoints instead of booking short taxi hops. See the bike rental page linked below.

FAQ
Booking questions people ask before confirming
What is the Murudeshwar to Gokarna taxi fare?
The Murudeshwar to Gokarna taxi fare starts at ₹2,000 one-way for a sedan and ₹2,800 for an SUV such as an Ertiga or Innova. Tempo Traveller and same-day return fares are quoted on WhatsApp. Tolls, parking, night charges and waiting time are confirmed in your quote.
How far is Gokarna from Murudeshwar by road?
About 77 km from Murudeshwar Temple via Honnavar and Kumta on NH66. The drive usually takes 1.5 to 2 hours, a little longer in heavy monsoon rain.
What is the Gokarna to Murudeshwar taxi fare?
The same as the other direction: from ₹2,000 one-way in a sedan and ₹2,800 in an SUV. We pick up from Gokarna town, Kudle, Om Beach and Gokarna Road station. Send the pickup point and time on WhatsApp and we confirm the total before you book.
Is a one-way taxi or a same-day return better?
One-way is right if you are moving your stay to Gokarna. A same-day return is better if you are staying in Murudeshwar, because the driver waits and brings you back without you hunting for a cab in Gokarna.
Can the taxi drop me at Kudle Beach or Om Beach?
Yes. Om Beach has road access close to the sand. For Kudle the car stops at the top of the cliff road and you walk down the steps. Half Moon and Paradise beaches are reached on foot or by boat, not by car.
Is Gokarna Road railway station close to Gokarna town?
No. Gokarna Road station is roughly 10 km from the town and beaches, so plan a taxi for that stretch too, especially for early or late trains.
Can I stop at Apsarakonda Falls or Mirjan Fort on the way?
Yes. Both are close to the route. Tell us the stops when you ask for the quote so the waiting time is included upfront.
Is Uber or Ola available in Gokarna?
No, you cannot rely on Uber or Ola in Gokarna, and they do not operate in Murudeshwar. Pre-book a local taxi for the Gokarna to Murudeshwar run and for the return so the car is waiting at your stay or the station.
Can you pick me up at Gokarna Road station and drive to Murudeshwar?
Yes. Share your train number and arrival time and the driver waits at Gokarna Road station, which is outside Gokarna town. The drive to Murudeshwar is about 77 km. Sedan from ₹2,000 and SUV from ₹2,800 one-way.
Can I take an early taxi from Gokarna to Murudeshwar for Netrani scuba diving?
Yes. Tell us your dive operator's reporting time and we set the Gokarna pickup to allow 1.5 to 2 hours on the road plus a margin. Early pickups may carry night charges, confirmed in your quote. For a relaxed dive morning, the better plan is to move to Murudeshwar the evening before. Scuba starts at ₹3,499 per person.
Gokarna to Murudeshwar: cab, bus or train?
A cab is door to door in 1.5 to 2 hours, from ₹2,000 for a sedan. Buses usually cost under ₹250 but only a few run direct; otherwise you change at Kumta. Trains from Gokarna Road take about 55 to 65 minutes (10107 at 05:35, not on Sundays; 56615 at 15:41; 16596 at 18:36), but the station is outside town, so add an auto or taxi. Confirm train times on NTES. The Gokarna to Murudeshwar distance, bus and train options guide linked below compares all three.
Can you also book my room and Netrani scuba diving?
Yes. Our team is based in Murudeshwar and can arrange your stay, the scuba slot and the Gokarna taxi in one WhatsApp chat so the timings fit together.
