Two airports
Dabolim or Mopa: which Goa airport are you landing at?
Goa has two airports, and the difference matters for this transfer. Dabolim (GOI) is in South Goa near Vasco, about 206 km from Murudeshwar Temple, a 4.5 to 5 hour drive. Mopa (GOX, Manohar International) is in the far north near Pernem, about 238 km away and 5 to 5.5 hours, because the car first has to cross most of Goa.
Check the airport code on your ticket before you book. The published ₹5,500 sedan fare is for Dabolim. Mopa adds roughly 30 km and extra time, so we quote it separately on WhatsApp. You see the full number before you confirm.
The drive
Goa to Murudeshwar route: down the coast via Karwar, Kumta and Honnavar
From Dabolim the car heads south past Margao and Canacona to the Goa–Karnataka border near Karwar, and then follows NH66 down the coast through Ankola, past the Gokarna turn-off, Kumta and Honnavar to Murudeshwar. From Mopa the first stretch runs south through Goa to join the same road. It is a scenic drive with long river crossings and views of the sea, but town sections in Margao, Karwar and Kumta slow things down.
If the taxi needs an interstate permit or border tax to enter Goa, we include it in the quote so the driver never asks you for it on the way.
Break the journey
Good stops between Goa and Murudeshwar
Five hours is long after a flight, so most travellers take one meal break and one short stop. Tell us which ones you want when you book.
Palolem or Canacona in South Goa (about 142 km from Murudeshwar), for a beach lunch before the border.
Karwar (about 113 km from Murudeshwar), for Rabindranath Tagore beach or a seafood meal.
Gokarna, a short detour off NH66 if you want to see Om or Kudle Beach on the way.
Mirjan Fort near Kumta, a quick stop close to the highway.
Pickup flow
How the Goa airport pickup works
Send your flight number and landing time. We follow the flight, and the driver's name and phone number are shared before the day. After you collect your bags, call the driver and walk to the arrivals pickup area. For late-evening landings, a 5 hour drive means arriving in Murudeshwar late at night. Some travellers prefer to spend the first night in Goa and leave in the morning. Either way, night charges are confirmed in your quote.
Ola and Uber do not operate in Murudeshwar, so even if you could find an app cab at the Goa end, the return or onward local travel still needs a local taxi.
Going back
Murudeshwar to Goa airport: when to leave
For a flight from Dabolim, allow 4.5 to 5 hours of driving, an hour of buffer and about 2 hours at the airport. That means leaving Murudeshwar 7.5 to 8 hours before departure. For Mopa, add another hour on top. If your flight is early in the morning, it is usually more comfortable to drive up the day before and stay near the airport.
Is Goa right for you?
Goa vs Mangaluru airport for Murudeshwar
Mangaluru (IXE, about 156 km) is usually the shortest airport transfer to Murudeshwar. Goa makes sense when you are already holidaying there, when flights from your city are cheaper or more frequent into Goa, or when you want to see Gokarna and Karwar on the way down. Many travellers fly into Goa and out of Mangaluru, or the other way round, to avoid driving the same road twice.
Plan the rest
Book the room and the Netrani dive with the transfer
After a long drive, you will want a room ready when you arrive. Netrani boats run in the morning and depend on sea conditions, so most Goa arrivals dive on the second day. Our team is based in Murudeshwar and can line up the transfer, the room and the dive slot in one WhatsApp chat. Send your dates and we suggest a plan, with no payment needed to ask.

How far is Murudeshwar from Goa airport?
About 206 km from Dabolim (4.5 to 5 hours) and about 238 km from Mopa (5 to 5.5 hours), both via Karwar on NH66.
Why does a Mopa airport pickup cost more?

Which route does the taxi take from Goa to Murudeshwar?

When should I leave Murudeshwar for a Goa flight?

Is Goa or Mangaluru the better airport for Murudeshwar?
Mangaluru, at about 156 km, is usually the shortest transfer. Goa suits you if you are already there, if flights are better, or if you want Gokarna and Karwar on the way.
