Murudeshwar Trip Plan: A 2-Day Itinerary with Netrani Island

Two days is the comfortable length for a Murudeshwar trip with Netrani Island. Spend day one on land: the temple, the 20-storey Raja Gopura, the 123-ft Shiva statue and the beach at sunset. Spend the morning of day two at sea, on the boat to Netrani for snorkelling or a try dive, and keep that afternoon free.

The order matters. Netrani boats report early in the morning and run only when the sea allows, roughly from October to May. Arriving on day one and sleeping in Murudeshwar means you are on the beach, rested, when the boat leaves.

Day 1: temple, Raja Gopura, Shiva statue and the beach

Morning darshan. The temple is open roughly from 6 AM to 1 PM and from 3 PM to about 8:30 PM, and entry is free. Timings shift on festival days; Karnataka Tourism's Murudeshwara page lists the current hours. Weekday mornings are the quietest, while weekends, Ekadashi and festival days bring long queues.

Dress for the temple. A dress code has applied since June 2025. Men are fine in trousers and a shirt, a kurta or a dhoti; women in a saree, or a salwar or churidar with a dupatta. Keep shorts, sleeveless tops and jeans for the beach. Footwear comes off at the entrance, and photography is restricted inside the sanctum.

Raja Gopura. The 20-storey gateway tower, 249 ft tall, has a lift to the top for a small fee. From up there you see the temple, the town and the sea on three sides.

Shiva statue and cave museum. The seated Shiva on Kanduka hill is 123 ft (about 37 m) tall. Beneath it is the Bhukailasa cave museum, a walk-through gallery of sculptures that tells the story of Ravana and the Atmalinga.

Lunch and a break. The temple serves free meals (anna prasada) in the afternoon, subject to availability, or you can eat in town. The temple's early-afternoon closing hours are a good time to rest.

Beach rides and sunset. Late afternoon belongs to the beach: a speed boat, a kayak, a jet ski, or parasailing when the wind allows. Prices and what each ride feels like are in our Murudeshwar water sports guide. Sunset over the sea is the day's main event, and the statue is lit after dark.

Before bed. Ask the booking desk for tomorrow's reporting time, keep dry clothes and a towel ready (our packing list helps), and eat a light dinner.

Day 2: the Netrani Island morning

Report early. Be on Murudeshwar beach 15 to 30 minutes before the time we send. A light breakfast helps, and so does a seasickness tablet if boats bother you.

The crossing. Netrani is about 19 km out, 45 to 90 minutes away depending on the boat and the sea. Nobody lands on the island; the boat anchors close to it.

In the water. Snorkellers float at the surface in a mask and life jacket, and no swimming experience is needed. Scuba beginners do a try dive with an instructor after a briefing and a medical questionnaire. To choose between the two, read snorkelling vs scuba at Netrani; for the dive itself, see the Netrani scuba diving guide.

Back on land. You are back on the beach in the afternoon; the exact time depends on the sea and the group. Keep the rest of the day loose and avoid booking a tight train or a long drive straight after. The boat timings page explains the windows.

If the sea is rough. The crew decides each morning. An unsafe day moves to the next workable departure without penalty, so in December and January a spare morning in your plan is worth having.

After the boat: go home, or add Gokarna or Kollur?

  • Gokarna is about 77 km north on NH 66, through Honnavar and Kumta, a drive of 1.5 to 2 hours. Its Mahabaleshwara temple and quieter beaches such as Om and Kudle make a good third day.
  • Kollur Mookambika temple is about 61 km away via Byndoor, around 1.5 hours by road.
  • Taxis for both can be booked on our sister site: Murudeshwar to Gokarna and Kollur and Murudeshwar.
  • Going home by train: the overnight Panchaganga Express to Bengaluru leaves Murdeshwar in the evening, and Konkan line trains towards Mangaluru and Goa run through the day. Our guide on how to reach Netrani Island covers each route.

How should you adjust the plan for your group?

  • Families with children or elders: go for darshan early on day one, before the queues. On day two, snorkelling suits most ages, or a private boat lets the family travel together at its own pace.
  • Non-swimmers: snorkelling needs no swimming experience. Try dives start from about 10 years of age and include a medical questionnaire; see Netrani scuba diving age limit.
  • Coming from Bengaluru by train: take the overnight Panchaganga Express out, which reaches Murdeshwar early in the morning, and the same train back on the evening of day two. That is one hotel night and two nights on the train.
  • Pilgrims: add Gokarna and Kollur for a three-day temple circuit, and avoid the Maha Shivaratri rush unless that is the reason for your visit.

Questions travellers ask

How much time is required for Murudeshwar Temple darshan?

On a weekday morning darshan itself is usually quick, but on weekends, festival days and around Maha Shivaratri the queues are far longer. Allow half a day for the temple, the Raja Gopura lift, the statue and the cave museum together.

Can I wear jeans in Murudeshwar Temple?

It is safest not to. The dress code in force since June 2025 bars shorts and sleeveless tops, and news reports of the temple notice list jeans too. Men can wear trousers and a shirt, a kurta or a dhoti; women a saree, or a salwar or churidar with a dupatta.

What is the distance from Murudeshwar to Gokarna?

About 77 km by road on NH 66, through Honnavar and Kumta, which takes 1.5 to 2 hours. Our sister site has a Gokarna to Murudeshwar route guide with bus and train options.

Which beach is best, Gokarna or Murudeshwar?

They suit different trips. Murudeshwar has one long, lively beach beside the temple, with water sports and the boats to Netrani; Gokarna has several smaller, quieter beaches such as Om and Kudle. Many travellers do both.

Is one day enough for Murudeshwar and Netrani?

Only if you are already in town the night before. The Netrani boat takes the morning and part of the afternoon, so the temple and the beach need another half-day; two days is comfortable.

When is the best time of year for this trip?

October to May, when the Netrani boats run; December to February usually brings the calmest sea. See Netrani Island season and best time for month-by-month notes.
