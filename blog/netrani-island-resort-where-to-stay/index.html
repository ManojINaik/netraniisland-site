Netrani Island Resort and Stay: Where to Sleep for the Boat Trip

By Netrani Desk · Updated 16 November 2026 · 5 min read

There is no resort on Netrani Island, and no hotel, homestay or campsite either. Netrani is a small, uninhabited rocky island about 19 km off Murudeshwar. Visitors do not land: the boat anchors close to the island, you snorkel or dive from it, and everyone is back in Murudeshwar the same day.

So the stay question is really about Murudeshwar. Book a room there, ideally for the night before your boat, because Netrani trips report early in the morning at Murudeshwar beach.

If a booking app shows a resort or residency with Netrani in its name, check the address. It will be on the mainland, in Murudeshwar town.

Can you stay on Netrani Island?

No. The island rises straight out of the sea with rocky edges and no sandy beach, and nobody lives there. Karnataka Tourism's Netrani page notes that public landing is strictly prohibited, and tourist trips stay on the boat.

This is what a Netrani trip actually looks like:

  • You report at Murudeshwar beach in the morning for check-in, a briefing and life jackets.
  • The boat crosses about 19 km, which takes 45 to 90 minutes depending on the boat and the sea.
  • It anchors near the island while you snorkel or dive, then brings you back in the afternoon.

That makes Netrani a half-day trip from Murudeshwar, never an overnight one.

Where should you stay instead?

Stay in Murudeshwar, the temple town the boats leave from. It is compact: the temple and the 123-ft Shiva statue stand on a small headland, the beach runs north from it, and most rooms are in the streets between the temple and the national highway. Murdeshwar railway station is about 2 to 2.5 km inland.

Why stay the night before your boat?

Netrani boats leave early. The booking desk sends your reporting time the evening before, and you need to be on the beach 15 to 30 minutes ahead of it for check-in, the briefing and life jackets. The boat timings page explains the usual morning window.

  • A missed slot can cost you the trip. The seat, the boat and the crew are held for the morning, so if you miss the reporting time, a refund is not guaranteed.
  • Overnight journeys run late. The overnight train from Bengaluru reaches Murdeshwar early in the morning, and buses from Bengaluru take 10 hours or more. An hour's delay is enough to miss a boat; a night in town removes that risk.
  • A rested start makes a better day. You sleep properly, eat a light breakfast and take a seasickness tablet if you need one. Earlier departures also tend to meet a calmer sea.

After the trip you are back on the beach in the afternoon. If a long journey home follows, a second night saves you from rushing straight from the boat to a bus. Our 2-day Murudeshwar and Netrani plan shows how the two days fit together.

What should you ask the hotel?

  • An early breakfast, or a packed snack, on boat day.
  • Somewhere to leave luggage and take a shower after you return, if you check out that morning.
  • How far the room is from the beach. If it is not walkable, we can add a hotel pickup in Murudeshwar; add-ons start from ₹450.
  • Parking, if you are driving.
  • Hot water, and AC or non-AC.

Pack light for the boat itself: water, a towel, dry clothes and a pouch for your phone. Our packing list covers the rest.

Can you stay in Gokarna, Bhatkal or Udupi instead?

You can, but the early report makes it harder. Bhatkal is about 22 km south of Murudeshwar, an easy drive. Gokarna is about 77 km north (1.5 to 2 hours) and Udupi about 102 km south (2 to 2.5 hours), so from either you would leave before dawn to reach the beach on time.

Most travellers based there move to Murudeshwar for the night before the boat. Our guide on how to reach Netrani Island covers each route.

Travelling as a family or a group?

Families and groups of six or more can book a private boat: your own boat and timing, with divers and snorkellers together. Private boats cost from ₹18,000 for up to 6 guests, ₹28,000 for 10, ₹42,000 for 16 and ₹58,000 for 24. Colleges, companies and larger groups can use group bookings for separate boats and a single plan.

Questions travellers ask

Where is the island of Netrani located?

Netrani, also called Pigeon Island, lies in the Arabian Sea about 19 km (10 nautical miles) west-south-west of Murudeshwar, in Uttara Kannada district of Karnataka. It is reached only by boat from Murudeshwar beach, a crossing of 45 to 90 minutes depending on the boat and the sea.

Can I camp or spend a night near Netrani Island?

No. Tourist boats anchor near the island during the day and return to Murudeshwar the same afternoon, and visitors do not go ashore. Plan all your nights in Murudeshwar.

How early should I reach Murudeshwar for the Netrani boat?

Reach Murudeshwar the day before. The booking desk sends your reporting time the evening before, and you should be on the beach 15 to 30 minutes ahead of it. The boat timings page explains the usual morning window.

What is the cost of scuba diving in Netrani Island?

Scuba is quoted for your date, because instructor load, the mix of guests, the sea window and add-ons decide the price. Snorkelling starts from ₹2,200 per guest with the boat, gear and guide included. Compare the options on the pricing page.

Which month is best for scuba diving in Netrani Island?

Boats run roughly from October to May and stop in the monsoon. December to February usually brings the calmest sea and the best odds of clear water. Month-by-month notes are in our Netrani season guide.

Is it safe to do scuba diving in Netrani Island?

A try dive starts with a medical questionnaire and a briefing, and an instructor stays with you in the water. Trips are moved when the sea is unsafe. Read is scuba diving safe at Netrani before you book.

Can family members who do not dive come along?

Yes. Non-divers can snorkel from the same boat with a life jacket, and no swimming experience is needed. A private boat keeps the whole family together on its own schedule.
