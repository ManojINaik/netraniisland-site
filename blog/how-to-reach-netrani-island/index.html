How to Reach Netrani Island: Train, Bus and Road via Murudeshwar

By Netrani Desk · Updated 16 November 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answers

  • Netrani Island is reached only by boat from Murudeshwar beach, about 19 km and 45–90 minutes each way.
  • There is no public ferry; trips are booked and leave early in the morning.
  • Murudeshwar has its own Konkan Railway station, Murdeshwar (MRDW), about 2 to 2.5 km from the temple.
  • By road: Mangaluru about 157 km, Udupi 102 km, Goa (Madgaon) about 177 km, Hubballi about 205 km.
  • Arrive the day before your boat, so a late train or bus cannot cost you the trip.

There is only one way to reach Netrani Island: by boat from Murudeshwar beach, about 19 km offshore. The crossing takes 45 to 90 minutes depending on the boat and the sea. There is no public ferry and visitors do not land on the island; you go on a booked snorkelling or scuba trip that leaves early in the morning and comes back the same day.

So the real planning is getting to Murudeshwar, a temple town on the Konkan Railway and the NH 66 coastal highway in Uttara Kannada district. Aim to arrive the evening before your boat. For the sea leg itself, with the distance, crossing time and morning window, see our Murudeshwar to Netrani boat timings.

From Mangaluru (Mangalore) or Udupi

  • Train: the Matsyagandha Express, the late-night Netravati Express, the Madgaon–Mangaluru passenger and MEMU trains, the SMVT Bengaluru–Murdeshwar Express and the new Vande Bharat all stop at Murdeshwar. The faster trains take about 3 hours from Mangaluru and 1.5 to 2 hours from Udupi, and there are daytime options.
  • Bus: KSRTC and private buses run often along NH 66. Some stop on the highway rather than in town, so ask the conductor for Murudeshwar and expect a short auto ride in.
  • Car or taxi: NH 66 all the way north, through Kundapura, Byndoor and Bhatkal. Mangaluru city is about 157 km away and Udupi about 102 km. Our sister site arranges Udupi to Murudeshwar taxis and Mangaluru airport pickups.

From Goa

  • Train: Madgaon is 2 to 3 hours away on the same Konkan line. The passenger train and the Vande Bharat run in daytime, while the Matsyagandha and Netravati Expresses reach Murdeshwar in the small hours, so book a room for that night. The same two express trains start from Lokmanya Tilak Terminus (LTT) in Mumbai.
  • Bus: there are only a few buses, mostly leaving in the afternoon and evening, and they take about 4 hours from Madgaon. The train is usually easier.
  • Car or taxi: NH 66 south via Karwar, Ankola, Kumta and Honnavar. Madgaon is about 177 km away, Dabolim airport about 206 km (4.5 to 5 hours) and Mopa airport about 238 km (5 to 5.5 hours). See Goa airport to Murudeshwar taxis on our sister site.

From Hubballi (Hubli)

  • Train: there is no direct train from Hubballi to Murdeshwar, so go by road.
  • Car or taxi: about 205 km, 4 to 4.5 hours, crossing the Western Ghats via Yellapur or Sirsi and joining NH 66 on the coast. Drive the ghat sections in daylight where you can. Our sister site arranges Hubballi airport to Murudeshwar taxis.
  • Bus: buses take about 4.5 to 5 hours. Departures vary through the day, so check current timings with KSRTC or a booking app.

From Bengaluru

  • Train: the daily Panchaganga Express leaves KSR Bengaluru in the evening and reaches Murdeshwar early the next morning. The daily SMVT Bengaluru–Murdeshwar Express runs overnight the longer way, via Mysuru and Mangaluru, and arrives around midday. By day there is the Yesvantpur–Karwar Express on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays and, from 15 November 2026, a Yesvantpur–Madgaon Vande Bharat via Hassan and Mangaluru that stops at Murdeshwar every day except Wednesday, with chair-car seats only.
  • Bus: KSRTC and private overnight buses take around 10.5 to 12 hours.
  • Car: about 475 to 500 km, 9 to 11 hours. Our Bangalore to Netrani Island guide covers the drive and the overnight options in detail.

From Gokarna

Murudeshwar is about 77 km south of Gokarna on NH 66, through Kumta and Honnavar, a drive of 1.5 to 2 hours. Trains run from Gokarna Road station, which is outside Gokarna town, and take about an hour. To make an early boat, stay in Murudeshwar the night before, or book a Gokarna to Murudeshwar taxi for the previous evening.

What should you know about Murdeshwar station?

Murudeshwar has its own station, Murdeshwar (code MRDW), on the Konkan Railway, about 2 to 2.5 km from the temple by road. Autos wait outside, and our sister site arranges station pickups if you arrive early or with luggage.

It is a small station, and some long-distance trains on the Konkan line pass through without stopping, so check that your train actually halts at Murdeshwar before you book.

Train timings change too. On the Konkan line, trains run slower under the monsoon timetable (in 2026 it ran from 15 June to 20 October), and new trains are added, so check current times on NTES before you travel.

When should you arrive for the morning boat?

Arrive the day before. Netrani boats report early, the booking desk sends your exact time the evening before, and you need to be on the beach 15 to 30 minutes ahead of it. If you miss the reporting time, a refund is not guaranteed, because the seat, the boat and the crew were held for you.

  • On an overnight train or bus: use the day you arrive for the temple and the beach, and take the boat the next morning.
  • Driving from Hubballi or Bengaluru: do not drive through the night and go straight to the boat; sleep first.
  • Going home: you are back on the beach in the afternoon, so leave a buffer before your train or a long drive. The overnight trains to Bengaluru leave Murdeshwar in the afternoon and evening.

The last leg: Murudeshwar beach to Netrani

  • Where the boats leave: from Murudeshwar beach beside the temple. There is no jetty, so boarding is from the beach; wear sandals you do not mind getting wet.
  • Getting to the beach that morning: from most rooms in town it is a short walk or auto ride, and we can add a hotel pickup in Murudeshwar (add-ons from ₹450). If you drive, ask the hotel about parking; there is also a car park in front of the temple's Raja Gopura.
  • The crossing: about 19 km, 45 to 90 minutes each way depending on the boat and the sea.
  • At the island: the boat anchors nearby while you snorkel or dive, and nobody goes ashore.
  • What it costs: shared snorkelling trips from ₹2,200 per guest, including the two-way boat, gear and a guide; scuba quoted for your date; private boats from ₹18,000 for up to 6 guests. Compare on the pricing page.
  • Season: boats run roughly from October to May and stop in the monsoon; see Netrani season and best time.

Questions travellers ask

How do I go from Gokarna to Netrani Island?

Travel about 77 km south to Murudeshwar on NH 66, which takes 1.5 to 2 hours, ideally the evening before, and take the morning boat from Murudeshwar beach. Our sister site arranges a Gokarna to Murudeshwar taxi.

How do I go from Mangalore to Murudeshwar?

By train, in about 3 hours on the faster Konkan line trains such as the Matsyagandha Express; by KSRTC or private bus along NH 66; or by car or taxi, about 157 km and 3 to 3.5 hours. Check current train times on NTES before you travel.

Is there a Vande Bharat train to Murudeshwar?

Yes. From 15 November 2026 the Yesvantpur (Bengaluru) to Madgaon Vande Bharat, via Hassan, Mangaluru Junction and Udupi, stops at Murdeshwar every day except Wednesday. The older Mangaluru Central to Madgaon Vande Bharat does not stop there.

How far is Netrani Island from Murudeshwar beach?

About 19 km, or roughly 10 nautical miles, west-south-west of the temple. The boat takes 45 to 90 minutes each way depending on the boat and the sea; the boat timings page has the details.

Is there a public ferry to Netrani Island?

No. Every trip is a booked boat outing from Murudeshwar beach for snorkelling or scuba, leaving in the morning and returning the same day. For weekends, book about a week ahead.

Which is the nearest airport to Netrani Island?

Mangaluru International Airport, about 156 km from Murudeshwar by road, a drive of 3 to 3.5 hours. Goa's Dabolim airport (about 206 km) and Hubballi airport (about 205 km) also work.

How far is Hubli from Murudeshwar?

About 205 km by road, 4 to 4.5 hours through the Western Ghats via Yellapur or Sirsi. There is no direct train, so most people drive, take a taxi or go by bus.

Can we stay in Netrani Island?

No. The island is uninhabited and visitors do not land, so everyone stays in Murudeshwar. Our guide to where to stay for Netrani explains the options.
