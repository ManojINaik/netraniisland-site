Netrani Island trips run roughly from October to May. In the monsoon the boats stop: every year the Uttara Kannada district administration bans water adventure activities, scuba diving included, and the sea is too rough for the crossing anyway.

The best time for scuba diving at Netrani is usually December to February, when the sea tends to be calmest and the odds of clear water are highest. October, November, March and April are good too, with a little more change from week to week. May depends on the year.

Is Netrani open now? Yes. In mid-November 2026 the season is running, and boats go out on days the sea allows. The crew checks conditions every morning, and the booking desk confirms your trip the evening before.

When does the Netrani season open and close?

Closing for the monsoon. Each year the Deputy Commissioner of Uttara Kannada orders a ban on water adventure activities across the district, covering boating, sea sports and scuba diving. In 2025 it ran from 2 June to 30 September; in 2026 it ran from 1 June to 31 August. The dates change every year.

Reopening. Netrani boats usually restart in October, once the sea has settled and that season's permissions are in place. In 2023, for example, the district allowed diving from 4 October.

End of the season. Karnataka Tourism's Netrani page lists November to May, while a December 2024 newspaper report put the operators' permitted window at October to April. In practice, late April and May depend on that year's permission and on the pre-monsoon swell, so check with us before planning a May trip.

What is the weather like in Murudeshwar through the year?

The weather on land matters too, especially with children or elders in the group. Karnataka Tourism describes three seasons on this coast:

  • October to February: clear skies, a calm sea and temperatures of roughly 22 to 30 °C, with comfortable evenings. This is the peak season for the temple, the beach and Netrani alike.
  • March to May: hotter and more humid, though mornings stay manageable. Water sports carry on until the pre-monsoon swell arrives.
  • June to September: heavy rain, a green coast and a rough sea. Netrani boats and beach rides stop, while the temple stays open and quiet.

Monsoon travel is slower too. Trains on the Konkan line run on a reduced-speed monsoon timetable, which in 2026 ran from 15 June to 20 October.

What is the best time for your kind of trip?

  • First-time divers and snorkellers: December to February, for a calmer crossing and the best odds of clear water. Choose a weekday if you can, when the town is quieter. Read the Netrani scuba diving guide before you pick a date.
  • Families with children: the same window, but Christmas to New Year is the busiest time in town. February is a good compromise.
  • A quieter trip: March and April, when Karnataka Tourism notes visitor numbers drop and rooms are easier to find. Keep a day of flexibility, because the wind starts to build.
  • Pilgrims combining darshan and Netrani: any month from November to March works. Avoid Maha Shivaratri week unless it is the reason you are coming, as rooms fill weeks ahead.

What happens if the sea is rough on your day?

The crew checks the sea every morning before the boats go. If conditions are unsafe, your trip moves to the next workable departure at no extra cost, as the boat timings page explains.

Two habits make this painless:

  • Book the boat for your first morning in Murudeshwar, not your last. If the sea is rough, you still have a spare day.
  • Keep a weekday in reserve in December and January. Peak-season weekends are the busiest, so a weekday alternative makes rescheduling easier.

If you are wondering why a day gets called off at all, is scuba diving safe at Netrani explains how sea conditions decide it.

How early should you book?

This season has three dates to plan around: Christmas Day falls on Friday 25 December 2026 and New Year's Day on Friday 1 January 2027, so both make long weekends, and Maha Shivaratri is on Saturday 6 March 2027. Expect a full town around all three, so book early.

  • Boats: for weekends, book 5 to 7 days ahead where possible. For private boats, scuba-heavy groups and larger outings, 10 to 14 days ahead works better, as our FAQ advises.
  • Rooms: Karnataka Tourism advises booking at least two weeks ahead for Diwali, Christmas and New Year, and four to six weeks ahead for Maha Shivaratri. Our sister site, murudeshwar.co.in, checks rooms for your dates.

Questions travellers ask

Is Netrani Island open now for scuba diving?

Yes. The 2026–27 season is running, so for your date the question is the sea, not the calendar. Message the booking desk a few days before and we will tell you how conditions look and confirm the next workable departure.

Which month is best to visit Netrani Island?

December to February usually gives the calmest sea and the best chance of clear water. The full season runs roughly from October to May, and June to September is closed for the monsoon.

Is Netrani Island good for scuba diving?

Yes. It is a reef island where scientists recorded 92 fish species in a 2006 survey, with visibility of 15 to 30 m in good conditions. Beginners can do a try dive with an instructor; the Netrani scuba diving guide explains the day.

Can I visit Netrani Island in the monsoon?

No. Boating and scuba diving are banned in Uttara Kannada through the monsoon, so Netrani trips do not run. The Murudeshwar temple stays open all year, so a monsoon visit is for darshan only.

Is Netrani Island open in May?

Sometimes, mainly early in the month. The end of the season depends on that year's permission and on the pre-monsoon swell, so check with the booking desk before you plan a May trip.

Is Netrani Island open in October?

It usually reopens during October, once the sea settles after the monsoon. Early-October dates are the least certain, so keep them flexible.

What time of day is best for the Netrani boat?

Morning. The boats leave early, and early departures usually meet a calmer sea. The booking desk sends your exact reporting time the evening before.
