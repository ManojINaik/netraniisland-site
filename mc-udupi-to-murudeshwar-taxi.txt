Distance at a glance
Udupi to Murudeshwar distance by road, train and bus
Udupi to Murudeshwar is about 102 km by road and about 95 km by rail. How long it takes depends on how you travel:
By car or taxi: about 102 km on NH66, 2 to 2.5 hours door to door.
By train: about 95 km on the Konkan Railway, roughly 1.5 to 3 hours depending on the train and its stops.
By bus: KSRTC and private buses on NH66 take about 2 to 2.5 hours, plus the short ride from the highway stop.
Udupi Krishna Temple to Murudeshwar Temple: about 102 km, 2 to 2.5 hours by road.
Murudeshwar to Udupi: the same 102 km south on NH66.
The drive
Udupi to Murudeshwar route: 102 km straight up NH66
This is one of the simplest transfers on the coast. From Udupi the car heads north on NH66 through Brahmavar, Kota and Kundapura (Kundapura is about 66 km from Murudeshwar), past the beach at Maravanthe, then Byndoor (about 33 km), Shiroor and Bhatkal (about 15 km) before reaching Murudeshwar. The total is about 102 km and takes 2 to 2.5 hours.
There is no ghat on this route, which makes it easy for elders and children. The slow parts are usually town traffic in Udupi and Kundapura, and the occasional road works on the highway.
Pickup points
Pickups from Udupi city, Manipal, Malpe and the railway station
Udupi is spread out, so tell us the exact pickup point. We collect from hotels near the Krishna Temple, from Manipal (handy for students, parents and hospital visitors), from Malpe beach stays, and from Udupi railway station on the Konkan line. For a train arrival, share the train number so the driver can allow for late running.
By rail
Udupi to Murudeshwar by train
The 16585 Express leaves Udupi at 10:42 and reaches Murdeshwar at 13:50 (confirm on NTES). It is a comfortable daytime ride that gets you in by early afternoon, in time to check in and see the temple in the evening.
The Panchaganga Express, the Matsyagandha Express, the Netravati Express and the coastal passenger and MEMU trains also stop at Murdeshwar (MRDW). Their timings vary, so check NTES for your date. Murdeshwar station is 1.5-2 km from the temple and an auto costs ₹50-80.
By bus
Udupi to Murudeshwar by bus on NH66
KSRTC and private buses run frequently along NH66 from Udupi towards Kundapura, Bhatkal and Murudeshwar. Timings change, so check KSRTC for the latest schedule. Some buses stop on the highway rather than in town, so ask for the Murudeshwar stop and keep a short auto ride in mind.
Temple to temple
Udupi Krishna Temple to Murudeshwar Temple
From Udupi Sri Krishna Temple to Murudeshwar Temple is about 102 km by road and takes 2 to 2.5 hours on NH66. Murudeshwar Temple is open 6:00 AM-1:00 PM and 3:00 PM-8:30 PM, so time the drive to avoid the midday closure.
A morning darshan in Udupi followed by a late-morning start usually reaches Murudeshwar for the afternoon session, with time left for sunset by the Shiva statue.
Stops on the way
Udupi Krishna Temple, Malpe and Maravanthe stops
Because the drive is only a couple of hours, it is easy to turn the transfer into a relaxed half day. Add the stops when you request the quote so the waiting time is included.
Udupi Sri Krishna Temple and the surrounding Car Street, before you leave Udupi. Check the darshan timings for your day.
Malpe beach, about 6 km west of Udupi city. Boats to St Mary's Island run only in season and depend on the sea.
Maravanthe, where NH66 runs between the Arabian Sea and the Sowparnika river. A favourite photo stop.
Kundapura for lunch, roughly halfway.
The other way
Murudeshwar to Udupi taxi
Heading south instead? Book a Murudeshwar to Udupi taxi for the temple, a train from Udupi station or onward travel to Mangaluru. If you are catching a train or a flight, tell us the departure time and we suggest a pickup time with buffer. For Mangaluru Airport, the dedicated airport transfer is usually simpler than stopping in Udupi.
Ola and Uber do not operate in Murudeshwar, so book the Udupi-bound taxi in advance, especially for early-morning starts.
Timing tips
When to leave Udupi for Murudeshwar
A late-morning or early-afternoon start gets you to Murudeshwar in time for an evening temple visit and sunset by the Shiva statue. Murudeshwar Temple is usually open from 6:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM to 8:30 PM, so aim to arrive by 6 PM if darshan is on the plan. If you are leaving Udupi straight after a morning visit to the Krishna Temple, you can still reach Murudeshwar by early afternoon, check in and rest before going out.
In the monsoon, keep a little extra time for rain on the highway. On long weekends, expect slower traffic around Kundapura and Byndoor in both directions.
Temple add-on
Add Kollur Mookambika on the way
Kollur Mookambika sits inland from Byndoor, and many families travelling between Udupi and Murudeshwar like to include it. Going Udupi to Kollur to Murudeshwar adds a detour and temple time, so plan it as a full day and ask us for a combined quote. If you would rather keep it short, do Kollur separately from Murudeshwar, which is about 61 km.

Arrive in Murudeshwar with your room and dive sorted
Most travellers arriving from Udupi want to see the temple and the Shiva statue in the evening and dive at Netrani the next morning. Boats run in the morning and depend on sea conditions. Our team is based in Murudeshwar and can book the taxi, a room near the temple or beach, and the dive slot in one WhatsApp chat. Is there a train from Udupi to Murudeshwar?
Yes. The 16585 Express leaves Udupi at 10:42 and reaches Murdeshwar at 13:50 (confirm on NTES). The Panchaganga and other coastal trains also stop at Murdeshwar; check NTES for their timings.
Is there a bus from Udupi to Murudeshwar?
Yes. KSRTC and private buses run often along NH66 from Udupi towards Kundapura, Bhatkal and Murudeshwar, taking about 2 to 2.5 hours. Some stop on the highway, so keep a short auto ride to the temple in mind.
Can I visit Murudeshwar from Udupi in a day?
Yes. The drive is about 102 km and 2 to 2.5 hours each way, so a day trip by taxi works well. Plan around the temple hours (6:00 AM-1:00 PM and 3:00 PM-8:30 PM). If you also want Netrani scuba, stay overnight, because the boats report early in the morning.
How far is Murudeshwar from Udupi?
About 102 km by road on NH66 via Kundapura and Byndoor, usually 2 to 2.5 hours.
Is there a ghat road between Udupi and Murudeshwar?
No. It is the flat coastal highway all the way, comfortable for elders and children.
Can you pick up from Manipal or Udupi railway station?
Yes. Share the hotel, hostel, hospital or train number and the driver plans the pickup around it.

Can we include Kollur Mookambika on the way?
Yes, as a full-day plan with a detour from Byndoor. Ask for a combined quote. Kollur to Murudeshwar alone is about 61 km.
Are Ola or Uber available for this trip?
