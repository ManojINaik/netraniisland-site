Murdeshwar (MRDW)
 on the Konkan Railway, 1.5-2 km from the temple. From Bengaluru take the
16595 Panchaganga Express
 (KSR 18:50, arrives 06:04). From Mumbai take the 
12619 Matsyagandha Express
 from LTT, Thane or Panvel (arrives 05:34); nothing from CSMT stops here. From Pune there is the weekly
11097 Poorna Express
. From Goa, the Netravati, Matsyagandha and coastal passenger trains all stop here.

Trains to Murudeshwar from your city
From Bengaluru
Bengaluru has the most choice. The 16595 Panchaganga Express is the fastest: it leaves KSR Bengaluru at 18:50 (Yesvantpur 19:02) and reaches Murdeshwar at 06:04, about 11 hours. That gives you a full first day for darshan and the beach, and it is the train to book first.
The 16585 SMVT Bengaluru-Murdeshwar Express leaves SMVT at 19:45 (KSR 20:25), runs the longer way via Mysuru, Mangaluru and Udupi, and reaches Murdeshwar at 13:50. It suits travellers boarding at Mysuru or who could not get Panchaganga berths.
For a daytime journey through the Western Ghats, the 16515 Yesvantpur-Karwar Express with its glass-roof Vistadome coach leaves Yesvantpur at 07:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday and reaches Murdeshwar at 20:10.

From Mumbai (LTT, Thane, Panvel)
Two daily trains run direct on the Konkan Railway. The 12619 Matsyagandha Express leaves Lokmanya Tilak Terminus (LTT) at 15:10 and reaches Murdeshwar at 05:34; the 16345 Netravati Express leaves LTT at 11:40 and arrives at 02:20. Both also stop at Thane and Panvel.
No train from Mumbai CSMT stops at Murdeshwar. If you search from CSMT you will only see trains that pass through without stopping, so search from LTT, Thane or Panvel instead.

From Goa (Madgaon, Karmali, Thivim)
Goa is on the same Konkan line, so you have several options. The 16345 Netravati stops at Thivim, Karmali and Madgaon, and Madgaon to Murdeshwar is about 2.5 hours. The 12619 Matsyagandha and the weekly Poorna Express also run via Madgaon.
For a cheap daytime hop, the Madgaon-Mangaluru passenger and MEMU trains (56615/56616 and 10107/10108) stop at Karwar, Gokarna Road and Murdeshwar. The MEMU does not run on Sundays and times vary, so check NTES.

From Pune
The only direct train is the weekly 11097 Poorna Express: Pune 22:50 on Saturday, via Satara, Miraj, Belagavi and Madgaon, reaching Murdeshwar at 16:02 on Sunday. On other days, Pune travellers usually reach Panvel and board the Matsyagandha or Netravati, or travel by overnight bus.

From Hyderabad
The 12789 Kacheguda-Kumta Superfast runs twice a week from Kacheguda at 06:05 and reaches Murdeshwar at 13:36 the next day. It is direct, but it goes the long way round via the Kerala coast and takes about 31 hours.
Most Hyderabad travellers are faster going by road (710-780 km, 13-15 hours), or by train or flight to Bengaluru and then the overnight Panchaganga.

From Chennai
There is no direct train from Chennai. The usual plan is a daytime train to KSR Bengaluru that arrives with time to spare before the 18:50 Panchaganga, or an evening connection to the 16585 from SMVT/KSR. The alternative is an overnight train to Mangaluru and a coastal train or a 156-159 km taxi north.

From Kerala, Mangaluru and Udupi
Northbound from Kerala, the 16346 Netravati stops at Murdeshwar at 01:00 on its way to Mumbai, and the Kacheguda-Kumta 12789 also comes up the coast. From Mangaluru and Udupi you can use the 16585 (which arrives at 13:50), the 12620 Matsyagandha, or the passenger and MEMU trains towards Madgaon.
Udupi is only about 102 km by road, so a taxi is often quicker door to door for families.

From Delhi, Ahmedabad and Hubballi
There is no direct train from Delhi or Ahmedabad that stops at Murdeshwar. Typical options are a train to Mumbai and then the Matsyagandha or Netravati from LTT or Panvel, or a long-distance Konkan train to Madgaon followed by a coastal train or a taxi (Madgaon is about 177 km by road).
From Hubballi, the road is the practical route: about 205 km and 4-4.5 hours via the ghats. Flying into Hubballi and taking a taxi works well too.

Arriving at Murdeshwar station: what to expect
Murdeshwar is a small station on the Konkan line, about 1.5-2 km inland from the temple and beach. Autos wait for most trains and usually charge ₹50-80 to the temple area; a private taxi is about ₹150-200. Ola and Uber do not operate in Murudeshwar.
The catch is the timing. The Netravati arrives at 02:20, the Matsyagandha at 05:34 and the Panchaganga at 06:04. At those hours there are fewer autos, and most hotels will not have your room ready unless you asked for early check-in in advance. Two small steps fix this: book a
station-to-temple pickup
with a driver who tracks your train, and mention your arrival time when you book
your room in Murudeshwar
. Temple darshan opens at 6:00 AM, so an early arrival can go straight into a quiet morning visit.
Planning a Netrani dive? Boats leave in the morning and run only in suitable sea conditions, so do not plan the dive on the morning you arrive at 05:34. Keep it for the next day and see

Going back: return trains from Murdeshwar
To Bengaluru, the 16596 Panchaganga departs at 19:32 (reaches KSR 07:15) and the 16586 departs at 14:25 (reaches SMVT 07:15 via Mysuru). To Mumbai, the 12620 Matsyagandha departs at 15:48 (reaches LTT 06:35) and the 16346 Netravati at 01:00. To Pune, the weekly 11098 Poorna departs at 05:20 on Tuesday. To Hyderabad, the 12790 departs at 16:04. The evening departures let you fit in a last darshan; ask your hotel for a late checkout if your train leaves in the afternoon. More detail is on the

Common Questions
Trains to Murudeshwar: FAQs
Station code, best trains by city, arrival times and return trains.
What is the railway station code for Murudeshwar?
Murudeshwar's station is called Murdeshwar, code MRDW. It is on the Konkan Railway line between Bhatkal and Honnavar, about 1.5-2 km from the temple and beach.
Which is the best train from Bangalore to Murudeshwar?
The 16595 Panchaganga Express is the fastest: KSR Bengaluru 18:50, Yesvantpur 19:02, arriving Murdeshwar 06:04 (about 11 hours). The 16585 via Mysuru arrives at 13:50, and the 16515 Vistadome runs in the daytime three days a week.
Is there a direct train from Mumbai to Murudeshwar?
Yes, two daily: the 12619 Matsyagandha Express (LTT 15:10, arrives 05:34) and the 16345 Netravati Express (LTT 11:40, arrives 02:20). Board at LTT, Thane or Panvel. No train from Mumbai CSMT stops at Murdeshwar.
Is there a train from Pune to Murudeshwar?
Only the weekly 11097 Poorna Express, which leaves Pune at 22:50 on Saturday and reaches Murdeshwar at 16:02 on Sunday. The return 11098 departs Murdeshwar at 05:20 on Tuesday.
Is there a direct train from Hyderabad or Chennai?
Hyderabad has the twice-weekly 12789 Kacheguda-Kumta Superfast, but it takes about 31 hours. Chennai has no direct train; go via KSR Bengaluru and the Panchaganga, or via Mangaluru.
How far is Murudeshwar railway station from the temple?
About 1.5-2 km. An auto usually costs ₹50-80 and a private taxi about ₹150-200. Ola and Uber do not operate in Murudeshwar.
Trains arrive very early. What should I do on arrival?
Several trains arrive at night or before dawn (02:20, 05:34, 06:04), when autos are few. Pre-book a station pickup and ask your hotel for early check-in when you book the room, so you are not waiting with luggage.
Which trains go back from Murudeshwar to Bangalore?
The 16596 Panchaganga departs Murdeshwar at 19:32 and reaches KSR Bengaluru at 07:15. The 16586 departs at 14:25 and reaches SMVT Bengaluru at 07:15 via Mysuru. The 16516 Vistadome departs at 06:52 on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.
Are these train timings final?
They were checked against public timetables in October 2026, but Indian Railways revises timetables and runs special trains. Always confirm on IRCTC or NTES before booking.
