Murudeshwar to Gokarna and Gokarna to Murudeshwar: Both Directions
The two towns sit on the same stretch of the Uttara Kannada coast, with Gokarna to the north and Murudeshwar to the south. Whichever way you travel, you use the same highway, pass the same towns and cover the same distance.
Gokarna → Murudeshwar:
 leave Gokarna, join NH66 and head south through Kumta and Honnavar. Murudeshwar comes up after Honnavar.
Murudeshwar → Gokarna:
 head north on NH66 through Honnavar and Kumta, then turn off the highway towards Gokarna town and its beaches.
Gokarna town is a short detour off the highway, so allow a few extra minutes if your homestay is out near Om Beach or Kudle Beach.
The Route via Kumta and Honnavar, with Stops Worth Making
This is one of the prettiest short drives on the Karnataka coast. Rivers meet the sea, paddy fields run up to the road, and there are good reasons to stop. Here is the order you meet them heading south:
Mirjan Fort, near Kumta.
 A laterite fort with moss-covered walls and wells, set a little inland from the highway. Morning light is lovely for photos, and it rarely feels crowded.
Kumta and Kumta beach.
 Kumta is about 45 km from Murudeshwar and is the main town on the route. The beach is quiet and local, a good place to stretch your legs or stop for a cup of tea.
Honnavar and the Sharavathi backwaters.
 Honnavar is about 26 km from Murudeshwar. The Sharavathi river meets the sea here, and the bridge views over the backwaters are worth slowing down for. Ask locally about a short boat ride on the river.
Apsarakonda Falls, near Honnavar.
 A small waterfall with a pool and a viewpoint over the sea, a short detour from the highway. It has the most water just after the monsoon.
Idagunji Mahaganapati Temple.
 About 19 km from Murudeshwar, a little inland. Many pilgrims pair Idagunji with Murudeshwar on the same day. If you are already in Murudeshwar, the 
Idagunji temple taxi from Murudeshwar
 starts at ₹1,000.
With two or three of these stops, the 77 km becomes a relaxed half-day rather than a straight transfer.
Gokarna to Murudeshwar by Bus
Direct buses between Gokarna and Murudeshwar are 
few
. A handful run from Gokarna or Gokarna Cross and take about 1 hour 20 minutes to 1 hour 50 minutes, usually for under ₹250.
If you miss a direct bus, the usual way is:
Take a bus from Gokarna to 
Kumta
.
Change at Kumta for a southbound bus that stops at 
Murudeshwar
.
The same works in reverse for Murudeshwar to Gokarna: bus to Kumta, then change for Gokarna. Ask the conductor to confirm the Murudeshwar stop, and keep some buffer because timings vary through the day. Travelling with heavy bags, small children or elders? The change at Kumta is where buses get tiring, and that is usually when a shared taxi starts to make sense.

Gokarna to Murudeshwar by Bike or Scooter
Riding the coast is a joy in the cooler months. The highway is mostly straightforward, but it carries trucks and buses, so ride in daylight, wear a helmet and avoid the heaviest rain in the monsoon. The stops above are all easy to reach on two wheels.
If you are based in Murudeshwar and want to ride up to Gokarna for the day,

 and mention your route when you ask for a quote.
Is Uber or Ola Available in Gokarna or Murudeshwar?
No. 
Ola and Uber do not operate in Gokarna or Murudeshwar
, so you cannot rely on booking a ride from your phone on the day. Autos are available for short local hops, but for a 77 km run between the two towns you need a pre-booked taxi, a bus or a train.
Pre-booking matters most on checkout mornings, early dive days and long weekends, when local drivers are already busy. Booking the evening before means the car is waiting at your door rather than you looking for one.
Is Murudeshwar Temple in Gokarna?
No. This is a common mix-up because the two temple towns are often visited together. 
Gokarna's main temple is the Mahabaleshwar temple
, in Gokarna town. 
Murudeshwar temple
, with its 123-foot Shiva statue and tall Raja Gopura, is about 77 km south of Gokarna in 
Bhatkal taluk, Uttara Kannada district
.
Both are in the same district and both are much loved by pilgrims, so many families do Mahabaleshwar darshan in Gokarna and then drive down to Murudeshwar the same day or the next morning.
Goa to Gokarna to Murudeshwar
Coming from Goa, Gokarna falls on the way. You drive south on NH66 through Canacona, Karwar and Ankola, take the short detour into Gokarna, then continue to Murudeshwar through Kumta and Honnavar.
From Madgaon, the road distance to Murudeshwar is about 177 km, and about 206 km from Goa Dabolim Airport. A night in Gokarna simply splits the drive into two easy halves, with the last 77 km to Murudeshwar the next day. For full Goa distances, trains and the return trip, read the 
Murudeshwar to Goa distance guide

Two-Day Gokarna and Murudeshwar Itinerary
Day 1: Gokarna and the coastal drive
Morning: Mahabaleshwar temple darshan in Gokarna, then Om Beach or Kudle Beach.
Lunch in Gokarna, check out.
Afternoon: drive south, stopping at Mirjan Fort and the Honnavar backwaters.
Evening: reach Murudeshwar before 8:30 PM for temple darshan and the lit-up gopura.
Night: stay in Murudeshwar.
Day 2: Murudeshwar, Netrani or Idagunji
Early morning: Netrani Island scuba or a boat ride (October to May, weather permitting), or a peaceful sunrise at the temple.
Late morning: Idagunji temple and Apsarakonda Falls.
Afternoon: Murudeshwar beach, Kanduka Giri and lunch near the temple.
Evening: head onward, or catch the 16596 Panchaganga Express from Murdeshwar towards Bengaluru (confirm on NTES).
Staying the night in Murudeshwar is what makes Day 2 relaxed. Rooms near the temple fill first on long weekends and in December-January, so send your dates early.

Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
Quick answers for this guide, based on the most common traveler queries.
What is the distance from Gokarna to Murudeshwar?
Gokarna to Murudeshwar is about 77 km by road on NH66 via Kumta and Honnavar. The drive takes 1.5-2 hours without long stops.
What is the Murudeshwar to Gokarna distance by road?
It is the same, about 77 km and 1.5-2 hours, heading north on NH66 through Honnavar and Kumta, then into Gokarna town.
Is there a direct bus from Gokarna to Murudeshwar?
A few direct buses run from Gokarna or Gokarna Cross, taking about 1 h 20 min-1 h 50 min and usually costing under ₹250. If none fits your time, take a bus to Kumta and change there for Murudeshwar.
Which trains run from Gokarna Road to Murudeshwar?
The 10107 MEMU (05:35, not on Sundays), the 56615 Passenger (15:41) and the 16596 Panchaganga Express (18:36) leave Gokarna Road and reach Murdeshwar in about an hour. Confirm times on NTES before you travel.
How much is a taxi from Gokarna to Murudeshwar?
Published one-way fares start at ₹2,000 for a sedan and ₹2,800 for an SUV. Tolls, parking, night charges and waiting time are confirmed in your quote.
Is Uber or Ola available in Gokarna?
No. Ola and Uber do not operate in Gokarna or Murudeshwar. Pre-book a taxi, or use the bus or train.
Is Murudeshwar temple in Gokarna?
No. Gokarna's main temple is Mahabaleshwar. Murudeshwar temple is about 77 km south, in Bhatkal taluk of Uttara Kannada district.
What are the places to visit between Gokarna and Murudeshwar?
Mirjan Fort near Kumta, Kumta beach, the Sharavathi backwaters at Honnavar, Apsarakonda Falls near Honnavar and Idagunji temple, about 19 km from Murudeshwar.
Can I visit Gokarna and Murudeshwar in one day?
Yes, if you start early and keep stops short. Reach Murudeshwar before 1 PM or after 3 PM, when the temple is open. Netrani scuba needs its own early morning, so add a night for that.
What are the Murudeshwar temple timings?
The temple is open 6:00 AM-1:00 PM and 3:00 PM-8:30 PM.
