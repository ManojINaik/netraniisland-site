Distance and time
Kollur to Murudeshwar distance: about 61 km, 1.5 hours via Byndoor
Older guides often list Kollur as 85 km or more from Murudeshwar. The usual road route is shorter. From Kollur the road runs down through forest and farmland to Byndoor on NH66, then north past Shiroor and Bhatkal to Murudeshwar. That is about 61 km and roughly 1.5 hours of driving.
The Kollur end has a few bends through the forest, but there is no steep ghat on this route, and most of the second half is on the coastal highway. Kollur to Murudeshwar is one of the most common temple transfers on this stretch of coast, so drivers know it well.
Kollur Mookambika Temple to Murudeshwar Temple: about 61 km, around 1.5 hours.
Murudeshwar to Kollur: the same 61 km in reverse, via Bhatkal and Byndoor.
Kollur to Byndoor: about 29 km, the inland stretch through forest and farmland.
Byndoor to Murudeshwar: about 32 km north on NH66.
Without a taxi
Kollur to Murudeshwar by bus and by train
By bus: a few direct buses run, but the usual way is to change at Byndoor. Take a KSRTC or private bus from Kollur down to Byndoor (about 29 km, 40-45 minutes), then any bus heading north on NH66 towards Bhatkal and Murudeshwar (about 32 km). Allow 2.5 to 3 hours with the wait at Byndoor, and check the last bus if you travel in the evening.
By train: the nearest station to Kollur is Mookambika Road Byndoor (BYNR) on the Konkan Railway, about 30 km from the temple. Trains from there reach Murdeshwar (MRDW) in under an hour, but only a few run each day, so check NTES for your date. Murdeshwar station is about 2 km from the temple and an auto costs ₹50-80.
With elders, luggage or a fixed darshan time, a direct taxi skips both changes and is usually the quicker option.
Both directions
Kollur to Murudeshwar, or Murudeshwar to Kollur
Coming from Kollur: many pilgrims reach Kollur first, often by bus or train via Kundapura or Byndoor, and then want a direct taxi to Murudeshwar for the Shiva temple, the beach and a night's stay. Send us your Kollur lodge or temple pickup point and time.
Going from Murudeshwar: if you are staying in Murudeshwar, you can visit Kollur and either stay there or come back the same day. A same-day return includes waiting time while you have darshan, and the quote states it clearly before you confirm.
Temple circuits
Kollur, Murudeshwar and Idagunji in one day
The most popular pilgrimage plan on this coast links three temples. Kollur Mookambika, Murudeshwar and Idagunji Mahaganapati fit in one long day by taxi, because Idagunji is only about 19 km from Murudeshwar, near Honnavar.
If you have two days, add Gokarna Mahabaleshwar (about 77 km north of Murudeshwar) and stay the night in Murudeshwar between the temples. Send us your list and we suggest a realistic order. Tight plans with many temples usually lose the most time to queues, not to driving.
Early morning: darshan at Kollur Mookambika, then breakfast.
Late morning: drive about 61 km to Murudeshwar via Byndoor.
Midday break: Murudeshwar Temple, the Shiva statue and lunch by the beach.
Afternoon: Idagunji Mahaganapati, about 30 minutes north, then back to your stay.
Darshan timing
Plan around temple timings, not only distance
Temples on this coast close for a few hours in the afternoon, and special pujas or festival days change the timings. Check the current darshan timings for Kollur and Idagunji close to your travel date, and share them with us so the pickup time works around them. Murudeshwar Temple is usually open from 6:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM to 8:30 PM.
Weekends, school holidays and Navaratri bring longer queues at Kollur. On those days, start earlier and keep the plan to two temples rather than three.
Choose the vehicle
Sedan, SUV or Tempo Traveller for a temple trip
A sedan is comfortable for up to four people with light bags. Families travelling with elders often prefer an SUV such as an Ertiga or Innova for easier entry and more legroom on a long temple day. Groups from the same family or community usually book a 12-seater Tempo Traveller so everyone moves together. Only the sedan fare is published. SUV and Tempo Traveller fares are quoted on WhatsApp for your date.
Where to stay
Staying in Murudeshwar between temple visits
Murudeshwar makes a practical base. It has more room choices than Kollur, it is close to Idagunji and Gokarna, and you can add a beach morning or a Netrani boat trip. Rooms near the temple are limited in number and fill early on long weekends and around Maha Shivaratri. Send your dates and group size and we share options with the taxi plan, with nothing to pay just to ask.

What is the Kollur to Murudeshwar taxi fare?
A sedan starts at ₹2,200 one-way. SUV, Tempo Traveller and same-day return fares are quoted on WhatsApp. Tolls, parking, night charges and waiting time are confirmed in your quote.
How far is Kollur Mookambika from Murudeshwar?
About 61 km by road via Byndoor and NH66, roughly 1.5 hours. Older figures of 85 km or more are not the usual route.
How far is Mookambika Temple from Murudeshwar Temple?
Temple to temple it is about 61 km by road, around 1.5 hours by taxi via Byndoor and Bhatkal.
Is there a bus from Kollur to Murudeshwar?
A few direct buses run, but most travellers take a bus from Kollur to Byndoor (about 29 km) and change to a northbound NH66 bus to Murudeshwar (about 32 km). Allow 2.5 to 3 hours in total.
Which railway station is nearest to Kollur Mookambika?
Mookambika Road Byndoor (BYNR) on the Konkan Railway, about 30 km from the temple. Trains from there to Murdeshwar take under an hour, but only a few run each day.
Can I book a taxi from Murudeshwar to Kollur and back the same day?
Yes. The driver waits while you have darshan and brings you back. The waiting time is included in the quote you confirm.
Can I visit Kollur, Murudeshwar and Idagunji in one day?
Yes, with an early start. Idagunji is about 19 km from Murudeshwar, so all three temples fit in one long day by taxi. Queues on weekends and festival days can stretch it.
What are the Kollur temple timings?
Timings change for special pujas and festivals, so check the current darshan timings close to your date and share them with us. We plan the pickup around them.
Is there a ghat road between Kollur and Murudeshwar?
No steep ghat. There are forest bends near Kollur, then the flat coastal highway from Byndoor. It is comfortable for elders and children.
Which vehicle is best for a family temple trip?
A sedan suits up to four people. An SUV is easier for elders and luggage, and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller suits larger family groups.
Can you add Gokarna to the temple circuit?
Yes. Gokarna is about 77 km north of Murudeshwar. Most families do it on a second day with a night in Murudeshwar between the temples.
Can you book rooms in Murudeshwar for our group?
Yes. Our team is based in Murudeshwar and can arrange rooms, the Kollur taxi and local transfers in one WhatsApp chat.
