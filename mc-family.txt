Choose space and privacy together
A family room, two separate rooms and an entire-home stay offer different levels of privacy. Share how many adults and children are travelling, including children’s ages. Ask for the bed count and whether an extra bed is a cot or a floor mattress. For several rooms, say if being on the same floor matters and confirm that arrangement before booking.
Check what a homestay includes
A homestay request does not automatically include a private kitchen, home-cooked meals or the whole building. Ask which spaces are private and which are shared, whether cooking is allowed and what utensils are available. Check the bathroom arrangement and who to contact on arrival. For a beachside stay, request both the access route and the actual room view.
Make the stay easier for every generation
Tell us about lift or ground-floor needs, meal preferences, hot water and your expected arrival time. Families visiting the temple may prefer a manageable walking route; those spending more time indoors may prioritise room space and AC. If several vehicles or a larger cab are involved, ask about parking and drop-off. We can help combine your stay enquiry with local transfers.

Frequently asked questions
Can a family of five share one room?
It depends on the allowed occupancy and bed layout. Share adults and children’s ages so we can request a suitable family room or compare multiple-room options.
Can I request a homestay with a kitchen?
Yes. Specify whether you need your own kitchen or shared access. Cooking permission, equipment, cleaning and any charges must be confirmed in the quote.
Are meals and extra beds included?
Inclusions vary. Request the full family stay total with meal arrangements, children’s charges and each extra bed listed before confirming.
What is the price of a homestay in Murudeshwar?
Family homestay rooms were ₹1,200–1,500 a night when checked in late October 2026. December–January, long weekends and festival days cost more.
Is there a homestay near the beach in Murudeshwar?
Some homestays are a short walk from the beach. Ask for the walking route to the sand and whether the road is lit at night before you confirm.
Do homestays serve food?
Some serve breakfast or home-cooked meals on request. Meals are usually charged separately, so have them listed in your quote.
